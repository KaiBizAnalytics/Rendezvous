# Rendezvous — Go-to-Market Strategy
**Date:** March 2026 | **Market:** Vancouver, BC | **Horizon:** Year 1

---

## The GTM Angle: Win the First 48 Hours

Newly engaged couples Google "wedding planning where to start" within **48 hours** of getting engaged. That single search moment is the highest-intent, lowest-competition window in the entire wedding planning journey. No incumbent owns it — The Knot and WeddingWire are vendor directories; they win mid-funnel. Pinterest and Instagram are inspiration platforms; they win browsing. No one has built the product that captures that first anxious, excited, "now what?" search and turns it into a clear plan.

Rendezvous wins by being the answer to that question — not a directory to browse, but a concierge that takes your details and hands you back a picture of your wedding and a realistic budget in minutes.

**The GTM thesis: get to couples before anyone else does, and make the first experience so concretely useful that they never leave.**

---

## Phase 0 — Supply Before Demand (Weeks 1–4, Pre-Launch)

> **Rule:** Don't drive traffic to a half-empty platform. Couples who arrive and see thin vendor coverage will not return.

### Vendor seeding (the unsexy prerequisite)
- Lock in **50 Vancouver vendors** across four categories — venues, caterers, photographers, planners — before any marketing spend
- Every vendor must provide real pricing ranges (non-negotiable for listing)
- Target: 15 venues, 12 photographers, 12 caterers, 11 planners — enough for 5+ credible matches in every profile configuration
- Approach: direct outreach, position as "free listing, pay only when we send you a booked couple"
- Prioritize vendors with existing social audiences — they become co-distributors

### Content pipeline (plant SEO seeds before launch)
- Publish 6–8 foundational SEO articles targeting bottom-of-funnel Vancouver queries before launch, so they have indexing time:
  - "Outdoor wedding venues Vancouver under $15,000"
  - "How to allocate a $30,000 wedding budget in Vancouver"
  - "South Asian wedding photographers Vancouver"
  - "What does a Vancouver wedding actually cost in 2026?"
- Format: detailed, data-backed, visual — the kind of content couples bookmark and share

### Waitlist
- Simple landing page: "Tell us about your wedding. We'll tell you what it costs."
- Collect emails + basic profile data (city, date, budget range, guest count)
- Target 200 waitlist signups before launch day using the tactics below

## Phase 1 — Engagement Season Blitz (Months 1–2)

> **The unlock:** 40% of engagements in North America happen between December 24 and February 14. This creates a predictable annual demand wave. Rendezvous launches into it.

### The paid acquisition play: catch them before the search
- Meta/Instagram ads targeting: female, 25–35, in a relationship, Metro Vancouver, interests: weddings, engagement rings
- Ad creative: a real Vision Board output — "This is what your wedding could look like. Takes 4 minutes."
- Budget: $3,000/month in Month 1–2. Target CAC under $25 for a free account
- Retarget anyone who visits the landing page but doesn't complete the questionnaire

### Reddit — genuine, not promotional
- r/weddingplanning and r/vancouver — be present as a genuinely helpful resource, not a brand
- Answer real questions: budget allocation, vendor red flags, what to ask venues
- Drop the product only when someone asks exactly the question it solves ("I don't know where to start")
- One well-placed, genuinely helpful comment is worth more than any ad

### Pinterest — the highest-intent platform
- Pin Vision Board outputs (with couple permission) as "real wedding inspiration with real budget breakdowns"
- SEO-optimized boards: "Vancouver Outdoor Wedding · $25K Budget", "Boho Wedding Ideas · 60 Guests · What It Actually Costs"
- Pinterest drives long-tail discovery for months after publishing — plant now, harvest all year

### One strategic bridal expo
- Don't do all of them — choose one high-quality Vancouver bridal show (e.g., The Wedding Co.)
- Booth experience: live Vision Board generation from a visitor's questionnaire answers, on a large screen, in real time
- The demo IS the pitch — watching your wedding appear in 60 seconds is the most effective sales moment possible
- Collect emails in exchange for a free premium month post-launch

## Phase 2 — The Flywheel (Months 2–4)

> **The unlock:** The Vision Board is inherently shareable. Couples will show their AI-generated ceremony image to their partners, families, and friends. Each share is a referral.

### Vision Board as organic distribution
- Add a "Share my Vision" feature — couples get a branded link to their Vision Board
- Watermark is optional but subtle: "Planned with Rendezvous"
- Target: 1 share per 5 users = meaningful organic loop with zero ad spend

### Wedding planner partnerships — the trusted referral
- Approach 10–15 Vancouver wedding planners with this proposition: "Send your new clients to Rendezvous before your first meeting — they'll arrive with a budget breakdown, a vendor shortlist, and a visual of what they want. Your kickoff meeting gets better."
- Position Rendezvous as a pre-meeting prep tool, not a competitor
- Offer planners a co-branded referral link and a small revenue share on any premium conversion from their referrals

### TikTok content — "we tried AI wedding planning"
- Format: couple tries the questionnaire, sees their Vision Board, reacts
- This format (authentic reaction to AI output) performs exceptionally well
- Cost: zero, if you can find one couple to film it in exchange for free premium access
- One video with genuine emotion is worth $10,000 in ads

### Multicultural community outreach
- Vancouver has large South Asian and Chinese-Canadian communities — both significantly underserved by existing platforms
- Partner with South Asian wedding blogs, Mandarin-language community Facebook groups, LGBTQ+ wedding communities
- Content: "Planning a Chinese-Canadian wedding? Here's what the budget actually looks like for 150 guests in Vancouver"
- This is a long-term trust play, not a quick acquisition channel — start it early

## Phase 3 — First Revenue (Months 3–6)

> **The unlock:** Free users have experienced the "wow moment." Premium converts those who want to act on it.

### The premium unlock trigger
- Free tier: questionnaire + Vision Board + basic vendor shortlist (3 vendors per category)
- Premium ($19/month): full vendor comparison, unlimited shortlist, budget simulator ("what if I moved $2k from florals to photography?"), checklist, AI concierge (post-MVP)
- Trigger the upgrade prompt at the natural conversion moment: when a couple tries to add a 4th vendor to their shortlist
- Target: 10% of monthly active couples convert to premium by Month 6

### Vendor referral fees — the B2B revenue layer
- When a couple books a vendor they found through Rendezvous: $150 referral fee to the vendor
- Requires tracking: provide couples with a "contact this vendor" flow inside the platform (not an external link)
- Month 3: pilot with 5–10 vendor partners who opt in
- Month 6: roll out to all 50+ vendors

### First revenue milestone
- Month 3 target: $5,000 MRR (250 premium couples at $19/mo, plus early vendor referral fees)
- Month 6 target: $18,000 MRR
- These are conservative — if the Vision Board drives word-of-mouth as expected, growth will be faster

---

## Phase 4 — Deepen & Expand (Months 6–12)

> **The unlock:** You have traction, real reviews, and a product couples trust. Now systematize.

### Double down on SEO content
- By Month 6 you have real data: what budget ranges, venue types, and styles your couples actually plan
- Use this to create hyper-specific content that no competitor can match: "We analyzed 500 Vancouver weddings — here's what they actually cost by guest count and style"
- This kind of data-backed content earns backlinks, drives organic traffic, and builds category authority

### Vendor network effect
- By Month 6, vendors should be experiencing real lead quality from Rendezvous (budget-matched, intent-high)
- Ask top vendors for case studies + social proof
- Launch a vendor referral program: existing vendors refer new vendors, get one free month of featured placement
- Target: 100 vendors by Month 9, 150 by Month 12

### Prepare for Year 2 expansion
- Start conversations with Toronto and Calgary vendors in Month 10–11 (before you launch, repeat the Phase 0 vendor seeding)
- Build the playbook: what worked in Vancouver becomes the repeatable launch template
- Year 2 launch cities: Toronto (February 2027, peak engagement season), Calgary (May 2027)

| Pinterest | Month 1 onward | High intent; long shelf life per pin |

| Vision Board sharing | Month 2 onward | Organic loop; zero marginal cost |
| TikTok content | Month 2–4 | Viral potential; one-time effort for lasting reach |
| Bridal expo | Month 1 | One strategic event for demo + press + vendor leads |

## What This Is Not

- This is not a mass advertising play. Spending $50K on digital ads before the product has 100 reviews will burn budget without building trust.
- This is not a feature race with The Knot. Winning means going deeper on the "0→1" moment, not broader on feature count.
- This is not a vendor-first strategy. Vendors are supply. Couples are the product's reason to exist. Every decision should be optimized for couple trust first.
