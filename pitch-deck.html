Rendezvous 10
Competitive Advantages
Vision-First, Not Search-First
We give couples a picture of their wedding before they ask a single vendor question. No other platform does this. It's the only way to solve the 0→1 problem.
Intake-Driven Personalization
Recommendations are filtered against budget allocation by category — not total budget. $15k Venue ≠ $30k total. No competitor does constraint-first AI matching.
Price Transparency by Design
Vendors must provide pricing to be listed. Our model depends on it. The Knot and WeddingWire can't adopt this — it would kill their advertising revenue.
No Conflict of Interest
Vendors pay per qualified introduction, not for ranked placement. We can't be pressured to suppress reviews. Couples trust us precisely because our incentives are aligned.
Multicultural Vendor Matching
Cultural requirement tagging (South Asian, Chinese-Canadian, LGBTQ+-affirming) built into the data model from day one — a gap no incumbent has closed at scale.
Moat: The Vision Board
A couple's AI-generated ceremony image + budget breakdown is a personalized artifact they'll share, return to, and build from. Switching cost is their wedding plan itself.
hello@rendezvous.ca Structural differentiators — not feature parity plays.
